# Calibration handoff for Fiji v2.9

Highest-priority upgrades are: household dietary and waste evidence; customs
domestic/re-export physical flows; crop processing yields; Fisheries
species/product mass balances; comparable capture/aquaculture full costs;
stock-assessment-linked catch limits; and aquaculture feed, land, water and
wastewater constraints.

Until those are added, preserve the population-driven final-demand and open
backstop formulation. Treat normal import floors as continuing accounting
assumptions, not trade forecasts. Do not pin production to historical output or
force a capture/aquaculture share merely to reproduce a benchmark. Any future
constraint needs full-horizon physical evidence and the same control/candidate,
matrix, solve-time, residual, dual, shock and source-diff validation chain.
