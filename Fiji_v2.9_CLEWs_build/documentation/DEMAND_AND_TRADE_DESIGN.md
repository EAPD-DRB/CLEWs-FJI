# Population, final demand, imports and exports

For both crops and fish, resident food is the recent FAOSTAT per-person average
multiplied by annual UN population. The 2025 export quantity is added to final
demand and scaled with the same population ratio. The 2025 import quantity is a
minimum activity on an open import backstop; it does not reduce final demand.
If domestic supply falls, imports can rise above the floor.

Crop backstops output the existing crop commodities directly. Fish requires
new FSHRAW/FSHFOOD commodities so capture, aquaculture, post-harvest and imports
can close one common mass balance. No tourism demand is added.

This is intentionally a transparent accounting model. It does not solve for
world prices, trading partners, demand elasticities or export competitiveness.
