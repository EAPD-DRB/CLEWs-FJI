# Fiji v2.7 model structure

The active case uses JSON source parameters generated by MUIO. `genData.json` defines sets and object metadata; `R*.json` files contain parameter values. Permanent changes remain in these sources.

Land uses `MINLNDTOT` as the national source and 26 one-mode `LND...TOT` physical stock/class technologies. Five land stocks are fixed, 20 crop classes are endogenous under two resource ceilings, and `LNDOTHTOT` is the residual. `ENV_LAND` accounts for forest, grass/pasture, other/fallow and bare land. `ENV_WATER` accounts for the three water commodities. User constraints enforce national land, crop, irrigation, environmental-land and water closure.

Energy/crop/service overlays are mapped in `MODEL_MAP.csv`; unchanged parameter families inherit Fiji_v2.5 pending recovery of deeper upstream source lineage.

Fisheries adds three final useful-service commodities—capture propulsion,
aquaculture equipment, and post-harvest cold-chain/processing—and seven
one-mode physical conversion technologies using diesel, grid electricity, or
integrated solar equipment. Existing assets enter as declining residual
capacity; new capacity and activity remain endogenous. All Fisheries activity
bounds are open, and no policy target, historical carrier share, or observed
production value is imposed as a technology activity equality. The inherited
agriculture, industry, direct-electricity, and public-water boundaries are
reconciled at full precision to prevent double counting.

## Population-driven crop demand and trade (v2.8)

CRPCAS, CRPYAM, CRPCON, CRPOTH and SGCPROCFJI remain the final-demand
commodities. Five import technologies output directly to them. AAD is resident
food plus exports; TAL on each import technology is the minimum import floor.
No export technology or new crop commodity is required.

## Population-driven Fisheries demand and trade (v2.9)

Capture and aquaculture useful services feed FSHCAPHARV and FSHAQHARV to
produce FSHRAW. FSHPOSTPRC consumes FSHRAW plus post-harvest service and
produces FSHFOOD. IMPFSHFOOD also produces FSHFOOD. Only FSHFOOD has final
demand; the three useful services and FSHRAW are intermediate annual balances.
All four new technologies use mode 1 and one-year accounting capacities.
