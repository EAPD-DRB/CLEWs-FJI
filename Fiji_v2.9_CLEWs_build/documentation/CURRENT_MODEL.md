Fiji v2.8 replaced production-reproducing crop demand with resident food plus
exports and added five import backstops on existing crop commodities. Fiji
v2.9 adds FSHRAW and FSHFOOD mass commodities, three domestic mass links and
one fish import backstop. Crop and fish demand, imports and exports scale with
the UN WPP medium population pathway; tourism is excluded.

The model is technically valid and suited to exploratory demand and import
resilience screening. Aggregate annual capture is capped at 0.023661 Mt;
aquaculture follows a 2024–2028 programme ceiling reaching 0.001450 Mt and
then remains at that level. Both subsectors retain zero lower activity limits,
and imports cover remaining demand. This is a defensible screening mix, not a
nutritional, bilateral trade, stock-assessment, species-level or facility-level
forecast.
